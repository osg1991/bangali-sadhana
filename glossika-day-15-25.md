## Week 15: Directions & Places  

**Cultural Knowledge:** Bengali social life revolves around the *adda* (the long, unhurried chat among friends over endless cups of tea). Travel within a city is often by cycle-riksha.

### Functional Language: Asking Permission & Warnings
- **May I come in?** (ami ki bhetore aśote pari?)
- **May I sit here?** (ekhane ki bośote pari?)
- **Can I take a look?** (ekṭu dekhte pari?)
- **Go carefully.** (śabdhane jan.)
- **Don't touch that.** (ōṭa chũẏō na.)
- **Watch where you're going!** (dekhe col!)

### Situation Patterns
- **Go straight, then turn right.** (śōja jan, taropor ḍane.)
- **Which way is the bank?** (bêṅko kōn dike?)
- **The temple is beside the post office.** (mondir ḍakghorer paśe.)
- **Get off at this corner.** (ei mōṛe nam.)
- **The hotel is in the city center.** (hōṭelṭa śohorer majhe.)
- **The post office is on the right.** (ḍakoghrṭa ḍan dike.)
- **Turn left at the crossroads.** (cōurastaẏ bã dike jan.)
- **How much farther is the destination?** (gontobbo ar kot dur?)
- **Go through this alley.** (ei goli diẏe jaō.)

## Week 16: Hobbies & Free Time  

**Cultural Knowledge:** Food marks every festival. Winter brings *piṭha* (rice cakes), while *hilsa fish* is the centerpiece of celebration meals. On New Year, many eat *panta bhat* (fermented rice).

### Functional Language: Suggestions & Greetings
- **Let's go together.** (colun, ekśoṅge jai.)
- **Come on, let's walk a bit.** (col, ekṭu hãṭi.)
- **How about if we stay?** (kemon hoẏ jodi amra thaki?)
- **Hello.** (nomoskar.)
- **Peace be upon you.** (assalamu alaikum.)
- **What's up?** (ki khobor.)

## Week 19: Classifiers / Function Words

### Functional Language: Well-wishing
- **Good morning.** (śubho śokal.)
- **Happy birthday.** (śubho jonmodin.)
- **Take care.** (bhalō thekō.)
- **How are you?** (tumi kemon ach?)
- **I am well.** (ami bhalō achi.)

### Vocabulary
- **Classifiers:** টা (ṭa - general), টি (ṭi - polite), জন (jon - people), খানা (khana - flat), গুলো (plural marker), টুকু (a bit).
- **Other functional words:** ei (this), ōi (that), kōnō (any), śobo (all), kichu (some), proti (each), ar (and/more), ba (or), kintu (but), tai (so), jodi (if), tobe (then), je (that conj).

### Situation Patterns
- **Please give me a book.** (ekṭa boi din.)
- **Two people have come.** (dujon lōk eśeche.)
- **Whose thing is this?** (ei jinisṭa kar?)
- **All the students have come.** (śobo chatro eśeche.)
- **If I have time, I'll come.** (jodi śomoẏ thake, aśob.)
- **I don't know that man.** (ōi lōkṭake cini na.)
- **Every student got a book.** (prottek chatro ekṭa boi peẏeche.)
- **No one came except you.** (tumi chaṛa keu aśeni.)
- **Write with a pen.** (kolom diẏe lekhō.)

## Week 25: Emergencies & Help

### Situation Patterns
- **Help!** (śahajjo korun!)
